None of these were taken on the same day, but they've ended up filed together anyway — three walks through the same kind of green, spread out over about a month, none of them planned as anything more than getting outside for an hour. I only noticed the pattern going back through the folder afterward.

Both from the same walk, actually — the field on the way out, the tunnel of trees on the way back, maybe forty minutes apart. The light had already dropped by the second one; you can tell without me saying so.
